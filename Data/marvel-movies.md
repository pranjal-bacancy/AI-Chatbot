# Marvel Cinematic Universe — Reference Document

> Note: Box office figures in this document are approximate worldwide grosses, rounded, and can shift with re-releases. Film details were compiled from information available up to mid-2026; announced future titles and dates are subject to change.

## Overview of the Marvel Cinematic Universe

The Marvel Cinematic Universe, usually shortened to the MCU, is a shared fictional universe of superhero films produced by Marvel Studios. It is based on characters from Marvel Comics. The universe began in 2008 with the release of Iron Man and has since become the highest-grossing film franchise of all time.

The defining feature of the MCU is continuity. Characters, events and objects introduced in one film carry over into others, and the films are grouped into "Phases", with several Phases forming a larger "Saga". Post-credits scenes are used to tease upcoming films and connect storylines.

Kevin Feige is the president of Marvel Studios and the producer of every MCU film. He is widely credited as the architect of the franchise's long-form storytelling approach. Marvel Studios was acquired by The Walt Disney Company in 2009 as part of Disney's purchase of Marvel Entertainment.

Phases One through Three are collectively known as the Infinity Saga, a 23-film arc that concluded with Spider-Man: Far From Home in 2019. The central conflict of the Infinity Saga is the pursuit of the six Infinity Stones by the Titan warlord Thanos. Phases Four, Five and Six form the Multiverse Saga, which shifts the focus to alternate realities, variants of familiar characters, and incursions between universes.

Alongside the films, Marvel Studios produces streaming television series that share the same continuity. This document covers the theatrical films only.

## Phase One (2008–2012)

Phase One introduced the founding members of the Avengers in individual origin films and brought them together in a crossover event. It established the Tesseract, S.H.I.E.L.D., and Nick Fury's Avengers Initiative as connective tissue between otherwise standalone stories.

### Iron Man (2008)

Directed by Jon Favreau. Starring Robert Downey Jr. as Tony Stark, Gwyneth Paltrow as Pepper Potts, Terrence Howard as James Rhodes, and Jeff Bridges as Obadiah Stane.

Billionaire weapons manufacturer Tony Stark is ambushed and captured in Afghanistan, and is gravely wounded by shrapnel from one of his own company's weapons. Held hostage and ordered to build a missile, he instead constructs a powered suit of armour and an electromagnet to keep the shrapnel from reaching his heart, then fights his way out. Returning home, he shuts down his company's weapons division and builds a refined version of the suit. He discovers that his mentor Obadiah Stane arranged the ambush, and defeats him in a battle over Stark Industries.

The film ends with Stark publicly declaring "I am Iron Man" instead of adopting a secret identity. A post-credits scene features Nick Fury introducing the Avengers Initiative. Approximate worldwide box office: $585 million.

### The Incredible Hulk (2008)

Directed by Louis Leterrier. Starring Edward Norton as Bruce Banner, Liv Tyler as Betty Ross, Tim Roth as Emil Blonsky, and William Hurt as General Thaddeus "Thunderbolt" Ross.

Bruce Banner hides in Brazil, searching for a cure to the gamma radiation exposure that turns him into the Hulk whenever his heart rate rises. General Ross hunts him with a strike team led by Emil Blonsky, who volunteers for a super-soldier serum treatment to match the Hulk's strength. Blonsky's escalating enhancements transform him into the monstrous Abomination, and Banner must deliberately become the Hulk to stop him in Harlem.

This is the only MCU film in which Edward Norton plays Bruce Banner; the role was recast with Mark Ruffalo from The Avengers onward. Approximate worldwide box office: $265 million.

### Iron Man 2 (2010)

Directed by Jon Favreau. Starring Robert Downey Jr., Gwyneth Paltrow, Don Cheadle as James Rhodes, Scarlett Johansson as Natasha Romanoff, Mickey Rourke as Ivan Vanko, and Sam Rockwell as Justin Hammer.

The palladium core powering Tony Stark's arc reactor is slowly poisoning him, and he behaves recklessly as he faces his own death. Ivan Vanko, the son of a scientist his father betrayed, builds his own arc-reactor weaponry and attacks Stark publicly, then partners with rival contractor Justin Hammer. Stark synthesises a new element to replace the palladium, and with Rhodes in the War Machine armour defeats Vanko's drone army.

Don Cheadle replaced Terrence Howard as Rhodes in this film. Approximate worldwide box office: $624 million.

### Thor (2011)

Directed by Kenneth Branagh. Starring Chris Hemsworth as Thor, Natalie Portman as Jane Foster, Tom Hiddleston as Loki, and Anthony Hopkins as Odin.

Thor, the arrogant heir to the throne of Asgard, reignites a war with the Frost Giants and is stripped of his powers and banished to Earth by his father Odin. Living among humans in New Mexico and befriending astrophysicist Jane Foster, he learns humility. His adoptive brother Loki, having discovered he is himself a Frost Giant, seizes the Asgardian throne and sends the Destroyer to kill Thor. Thor sacrifices himself to save the townspeople, regains his hammer Mjolnir and his powers, and returns to stop Loki.

Approximate worldwide box office: $449 million.

### Captain America: The First Avenger (2011)

Directed by Joe Johnston. Starring Chris Evans as Steve Rogers, Hayley Atwell as Peggy Carter, Sebastian Stan as Bucky Barnes, Hugo Weaving as Johann Schmidt, and Tommy Lee Jones as Colonel Chester Phillips.

During the Second World War, frail Brooklyn recruit Steve Rogers is chosen for an experimental super-soldier programme and transformed into a peak physical specimen. Initially used as a propaganda mascot, he mounts a solo rescue of captured soldiers and becomes Captain America. He battles Johann Schmidt, the Red Skull, who leads the Nazi science division HYDRA and wields the Tesseract. Rogers crashes Schmidt's aircraft into the Arctic to save New York and is frozen for decades, waking in the present day.

Approximate worldwide box office: $370 million.

### Marvel's The Avengers (2012)

Directed by Joss Whedon. Starring Robert Downey Jr., Chris Evans, Mark Ruffalo as Bruce Banner, Chris Hemsworth, Scarlett Johansson, Jeremy Renner as Clint Barton, Tom Hiddleston, and Samuel L. Jackson as Nick Fury.

Loki arrives on Earth, steals the Tesseract from S.H.I.E.L.D. and mind-controls Hawkeye and others to help him open a portal for the alien Chitauri army. Nick Fury assembles Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, the Hulk, Black Widow and Hawkeye into a team that initially fights amongst itself. After Loki's forces attack the helicarrier and kill Agent Phil Coulson, the team unites for the Battle of New York, closing the portal after Iron Man carries a nuclear missile through it.

The first major MCU crossover and the film that established the team-up template. Approximate worldwide box office: $1.52 billion.

## Phase Two (2013–2015)

Phase Two dealt with the consequences of the Battle of New York, expanded the MCU into space with the Guardians of the Galaxy, and dismantled S.H.I.E.L.D. It also introduced the Infinity Stones as an explicit through-line.

### Iron Man 3 (2013)

Directed by Shane Black. Starring Robert Downey Jr., Gwyneth Paltrow, Don Cheadle, Guy Pearce as Aldrich Killian, Ben Kingsley as Trevor Slattery, and Rebecca Hall as Maya Hansen.

Suffering anxiety attacks after the Battle of New York, Tony Stark obsessively builds armour suits. A terrorist calling himself the Mandarin claims responsibility for a series of bombings, and Stark publicly challenges him, prompting an attack that destroys his home. Investigating, he discovers the Mandarin is an actor named Trevor Slattery, a front for Aldrich Killian and his unstable regenerative treatment Extremis. Stark destroys his suits and has the shrapnel removed from his chest.

Approximate worldwide box office: $1.21 billion.

### Thor: The Dark World (2013)

Directed by Alan Taylor. Starring Chris Hemsworth, Natalie Portman, Tom Hiddleston, Anthony Hopkins, and Christopher Eccleston as Malekith.

Jane Foster is infected by the Aether, an ancient force sought by the Dark Elf Malekith to return the universe to darkness during a rare alignment of the Nine Realms. Thor brings her to Asgard, and when Malekith attacks, Thor reluctantly frees Loki to help him. Loki appears to die saving Thor, and the two defeat Malekith on Earth. The Aether is later revealed to be the Reality Stone.

Approximate worldwide box office: $645 million.

### Captain America: The Winter Soldier (2014)

Directed by Anthony and Joe Russo. Starring Chris Evans, Scarlett Johansson, Sebastian Stan as the Winter Soldier, Anthony Mackie as Sam Wilson, Samuel L. Jackson, and Robert Redford as Alexander Pierce.

Steve Rogers discovers that HYDRA has secretly grown inside S.H.I.E.L.D. for decades and is about to launch Project Insight, a fleet of helicarriers designed to eliminate millions of people flagged as future threats. He is hunted by the Winter Soldier, a masked assassin who turns out to be his brainwashed friend Bucky Barnes. With Black Widow and Falcon, Rogers destroys the helicarriers and collapses S.H.I.E.L.D. entirely.

Widely regarded as one of the strongest MCU films and a political thriller in tone. Approximate worldwide box office: $714 million.

### Guardians of the Galaxy (2014)

Directed by James Gunn. Starring Chris Pratt as Peter Quill, Zoe Saldaña as Gamora, Dave Bautista as Drax, Vin Diesel as the voice of Groot, Bradley Cooper as the voice of Rocket, and Lee Pace as Ronan the Accuser.

Peter Quill, abducted from Earth as a child, steals a mysterious orb and is pursued by the fanatic Ronan the Accuser. Imprisoned alongside Gamora, Drax, Rocket and Groot, he forms an unlikely alliance with them to escape and sell the orb. The orb proves to contain the Power Stone, and the group defends the planet Xandar from Ronan, holding the Stone together to survive its energy.

The film moved the MCU firmly into space opera and was noted for its soundtrack of 1970s and 1980s pop songs. Approximate worldwide box office: $773 million.

### Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015)

Directed by Joss Whedon. Starring Robert Downey Jr., Chris Evans, Chris Hemsworth, Mark Ruffalo, Scarlett Johansson, Jeremy Renner, James Spader as the voice of Ultron, Elizabeth Olsen as Wanda Maximoff, Aaron Taylor-Johnson as Pietro Maximoff, and Paul Bettany as Vision.

Tony Stark and Bruce Banner use the artificial intelligence inside Loki's recovered scepter to create a global defence programme called Ultron. Ultron immediately concludes that humanity itself is the threat and sets out to cause an extinction-level event by lifting the city of Sokovia into the sky and dropping it. The Avengers, joined by the Maximoff twins and the newly created android Vision, evacuate the city and destroy Ultron. Pietro Maximoff is killed.

The scepter's gem is revealed as the Mind Stone, which is set into Vision's forehead. Approximate worldwide box office: $1.4 billion.

### Ant-Man (2015)

Directed by Peyton Reed. Starring Paul Rudd as Scott Lang, Evangeline Lilly as Hope van Dyne, Corey Stoll as Darren Cross, and Michael Douglas as Hank Pym.

Ex-convict Scott Lang is recruited by scientist Hank Pym to wear a suit that shrinks its wearer while increasing their strength. Pym needs him to steal the Yellowjacket suit from his former protégé Darren Cross before the technology is sold as a weapon. Lang infiltrates Pym Technologies, defeats Cross, and survives shrinking into the subatomic Quantum Realm by reversing the process.

A heist comedy in tone and smaller in scale than the surrounding films. Approximate worldwide box office: $519 million.

## Phase Three (2016–2019)

Phase Three fractured the Avengers, introduced Spider-Man, Black Panther and Doctor Strange, and brought the Infinity Saga to a close with the two-part Thanos conflict.

### Captain America: Civil War (2016)

Directed by Anthony and Joe Russo. Starring Chris Evans, Robert Downey Jr., Sebastian Stan, Anthony Mackie, Scarlett Johansson, Chadwick Boseman as T'Challa, Tom Holland as Peter Parker, Paul Rudd, and Daniel Brühl as Helmut Zemo.

After civilian casualties during an Avengers mission, governments propose the Sokovia Accords to place the team under United Nations oversight. Tony Stark supports the Accords; Steve Rogers refuses, particularly once Bucky Barnes is framed for a bombing. Helmut Zemo, who lost his family in Sokovia, engineers the team's collapse from within by revealing that the brainwashed Barnes killed Stark's parents. The Avengers split into two factions and fight at Leipzig airport, and Stark and Rogers part as enemies.

Introduces Black Panther and Tom Holland's Spider-Man. Approximate worldwide box office: $1.15 billion.

### Doctor Strange (2016)

Directed by Scott Derrickson. Starring Benedict Cumberbatch as Stephen Strange, Chiwetel Ejiofor as Mordo, Rachel McAdams as Christine Palmer, Tilda Swinton as the Ancient One, and Mads Mikkelsen as Kaecilius.

Arrogant neurosurgeon Stephen Strange loses the use of his hands in a car accident and, after exhausting conventional medicine, travels to Kamar-Taj in Nepal. Trained in the mystic arts by the Ancient One, he learns to manipulate time and space. He confronts Kaecilius, who seeks to deliver Earth to the dark dimension entity Dormammu, and defeats Dormammu by trapping him in an endless time loop using the Eye of Agamotto.

The Eye of Agamotto contains the Time Stone. Approximate worldwide box office: $677 million.

### Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 (2017)

Directed by James Gunn. Starring Chris Pratt, Zoe Saldaña, Dave Bautista, Bradley Cooper, Vin Diesel, Michael Rooker as Yondu, Karen Gillan as Nebula, and Kurt Russell as Ego.

The Guardians meet Ego, a Celestial who reveals himself as Peter Quill's father. Ego's true plan is to use Quill's inherited power to seed and consume thousands of worlds, and he admits to causing the death of Quill's mother. The Guardians destroy Ego's core, and Yondu, the Ravager who raised Quill, sacrifices himself to save him.

Approximate worldwide box office: $863 million.

### Spider-Man: Homecoming (2017)

Directed by Jon Watts. Starring Tom Holland as Peter Parker, Michael Keaton as Adrian Toomes, Zendaya as MJ, Marisa Tomei as May Parker, Jacob Batalon as Ned Leeds, and Robert Downey Jr. as Tony Stark.

Fifteen-year-old Peter Parker juggles high school with his ambition to become a full Avenger, mentored at arm's length by Tony Stark. He investigates a black market in alien weaponry salvaged from the Battle of New York, run by Adrian Toomes, the Vulture, who turns out to be the father of Peter's homecoming date. Peter stops Toomes without his Stark-built suit, and afterwards declines Stark's offer to join the Avengers.

Approximate worldwide box office: $880 million.

### Thor: Ragnarok (2017)

Directed by Taika Waititi. Starring Chris Hemsworth, Tom Hiddleston, Cate Blanchett as Hela, Tessa Thompson as Valkyrie, Mark Ruffalo, Jeff Goldblum as the Grandmaster, and Anthony Hopkins.

Thor returns to Asgard to find Loki impersonating Odin. The brothers' search for their father ends with Odin's death and the release of Hela, their previously unknown older sister and the goddess of death, who destroys Mjolnir and seizes Asgard. Stranded on the junk planet Sakaar, Thor is forced to fight the Hulk in the Grandmaster's arena. He escapes with Hulk, Loki and Valkyrie, and defeats Hela only by deliberately triggering Ragnarok, destroying Asgard itself while its people escape by ship.

A significant tonal shift toward comedy, and the film that gave Thor his more comedic characterisation. Approximate worldwide box office: $854 million.

### Black Panther (2018)

Directed by Ryan Coogler. Starring Chadwick Boseman as T'Challa, Michael B. Jordan as Erik "Killmonger" Stevens, Lupita Nyong'o as Nakia, Danai Gurira as Okoye, Letitia Wright as Shuri, Daniel Kaluuya as W'Kabi, and Angela Bassett as Ramonda.

After his father's death, T'Challa returns home to be crowned king of Wakanda, a technologically advanced African nation hidden from the world and powered by the metal vibranium. His claim is challenged by Erik Killmonger, the son of T'Challa's uncle, who was abandoned in Oakland and now intends to arm oppressed people worldwide with Wakandan weapons. T'Challa defeats him, and chooses to end Wakanda's isolationism.

Black Panther was the first superhero film nominated for the Academy Award for Best Picture, and won three Oscars. Approximate worldwide box office: $1.35 billion.

### Avengers: Infinity War (2018)

Directed by Anthony and Joe Russo. Starring Josh Brolin as Thanos alongside a large ensemble including Robert Downey Jr., Chris Hemsworth, Chris Evans, Benedict Cumberbatch, Tom Holland, Chadwick Boseman, Zoe Saldaña and Scarlett Johansson.

Thanos sets out to collect all six Infinity Stones, believing that erasing half of all life will bring balance to an overpopulated universe. He takes the Power Stone from Xandar and the Space Stone from the Asgardian refugee ship, sacrifices his adopted daughter Gamora to obtain the Soul Stone, and takes the Reality Stone from the Collector. The heroes split into groups to stop him on Titan and in Wakanda, and fail: Doctor Strange surrenders the Time Stone to save Tony Stark, and Thanos rips the Mind Stone from Vision.

The film ends with Thanos snapping his fingers and half of all living things turning to dust. Approximate worldwide box office: $2.05 billion.

### Ant-Man and the Wasp (2018)

Directed by Peyton Reed. Starring Paul Rudd, Evangeline Lilly as the Wasp, Michael Douglas, Michelle Pfeiffer as Janet van Dyne, Hannah John-Kamen as Ghost, and Laurence Fishburne as Bill Foster.

Under house arrest after Civil War, Scott Lang is pulled back in by Hank Pym and Hope van Dyne, who are building a tunnel to the Quantum Realm to rescue Janet van Dyne, lost there for thirty years. They are opposed by Ghost, who needs quantum energy to stabilise her own molecular phasing. Janet is recovered, but a post-credits scene shows Lang stranded in the Quantum Realm when the team operating the tunnel is erased by Thanos's snap.

Approximate worldwide box office: $623 million.

### Captain Marvel (2019)

Directed by Anna Boden and Ryan Fleck. Starring Brie Larson as Carol Danvers, Samuel L. Jackson as Nick Fury, Jude Law as Yon-Rogg, Ben Mendelsohn as Talos, and Annette Bening as the Supreme Intelligence.

Set in 1995, the film follows Kree warrior Vers, who is haunted by fragments of a life on Earth she cannot remember. Crash-landing in Los Angeles and teaming with a younger Nick Fury, she discovers she is human pilot Carol Danvers, that her powers came from an exploding experimental engine powered by the Tesseract, and that the Kree — not the shapeshifting Skrulls — are the aggressors. She turns on her mentor Yon-Rogg and leaves Earth to help Skrull refugees.

The first MCU film led by a female character. Approximate worldwide box office: $1.13 billion.

### Avengers: Endgame (2019)

Directed by Anthony and Joe Russo. Starring Robert Downey Jr., Chris Evans, Chris Hemsworth, Mark Ruffalo, Scarlett Johansson, Jeremy Renner, Paul Rudd, Karen Gillan, Don Cheadle, Brie Larson and Josh Brolin.

Five years after the snap, Scott Lang escapes the Quantum Realm and proposes using it for time travel. The surviving Avengers scatter across their own history in a "time heist" to retrieve the Infinity Stones before Thanos destroyed them. Natasha Romanoff sacrifices herself on Vormir for the Soul Stone. Hulk restores everyone Thanos erased, but a past version of Thanos follows them to the present, triggering a final battle at the ruined Avengers compound where the restored heroes return through portals.

Tony Stark takes the Stones and snaps Thanos and his army out of existence, dying from the effort. Steve Rogers returns the Stones to their timelines and chooses to stay in the past with Peggy Carter, handing the shield to Sam Wilson. Approximate worldwide box office: $2.79 billion.

### Spider-Man: Far From Home (2019)

Directed by Jon Watts. Starring Tom Holland, Zendaya, Samuel L. Jackson, Jon Favreau as Happy Hogan, and Jake Gyllenhaal as Quentin Beck.

Grieving Tony Stark, Peter Parker wants an ordinary school trip across Europe. Nick Fury drafts him to fight the Elementals alongside Quentin Beck, an apparent hero from another Earth. Beck is in fact a former Stark employee using drone-projected illusions to manufacture threats and position himself as the next Iron Man. Peter defeats him in London, but a post-credits scene shows Beck's edited footage framing Peter for the attack and revealing his identity to the world.

The closing film of the Infinity Saga. Approximate worldwide box office: $1.13 billion.

## Phase Four (2021–2022)

Phase Four opened the Multiverse Saga, dealing with grief and succession after Endgame and introducing a wave of new leads.

### Black Widow (2021)

Directed by Cate Shortland. Starring Scarlett Johansson as Natasha Romanoff, Florence Pugh as Yelena Belova, David Harbour as Alexei Shostakov, Rachel Weisz as Melina Vostokoff, and Ray Winstone as Dreykov.

Set between Civil War and Infinity War, the film follows Natasha as a fugitive reuniting with the undercover "family" she was placed with as a child. Together they destroy the Red Room, the programme that trained and chemically controlled the Widows, and kill its head, Dreykov.

Approximate worldwide box office: $379 million.

### Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings (2021)

Directed by Destin Daniel Cretton. Starring Simu Liu as Shang-Chi, Awkwafina as Katy, Tony Leung as Xu Wenwu, Meng'er Zhang as Xialing, and Michelle Yeoh as Ying Nan.

Shang-Chi, living quietly in San Francisco, is drawn back to his father Xu Wenwu, the immortal wielder of the ten rings and leader of a criminal organisation. Wenwu believes his dead wife is imprisoned in her hidden village of Ta Lo and intends to break it open, unaware he is being manipulated by a soul-devouring creature. Shang-Chi inherits the rings after his father's death.

Approximate worldwide box office: $432 million.

### Eternals (2021)

Directed by Chloé Zhao. Starring Gemma Chan as Sersi, Richard Madden as Ikaris, Angelina Jolie as Thena, Salma Hayek as Ajak, Kumail Nanjiani as Kingo, and Brian Tyree Henry as Phastos.

Ten immortal beings sent to Earth thousands of years ago by the Celestial Arishem reunite in the present. They learn that their true purpose was never to protect humanity but to allow it to grow until a new Celestial could emerge from the planet, destroying it. The group splits over whether to permit the emergence, and Sersi ultimately stops it.

Chloé Zhao had won the Academy Award for Best Director shortly before the film's release. Approximate worldwide box office: $402 million.

### Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021)

Directed by Jon Watts. Starring Tom Holland, Zendaya, Benedict Cumberbatch, Jacob Batalon, Marisa Tomei, Willem Dafoe as Norman Osborn, Alfred Molina as Otto Octavius, with Tobey Maguire and Andrew Garfield.

With his identity exposed, Peter Parker asks Doctor Strange to cast a spell making the world forget he is Spider-Man. The spell is botched and pulls in villains from other universes who died fighting other versions of Spider-Man. Rather than send them back to their deaths, Peter tries to cure them; Aunt May is killed in the attempt. Two alternate Peter Parkers arrive to help, and Peter finally asks Strange to make everyone forget him entirely.

Approximate worldwide box office: $1.9 billion, the highest-grossing MCU film outside the Avengers series.

### Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness (2022)

Directed by Sam Raimi. Starring Benedict Cumberbatch, Elizabeth Olsen as Wanda Maximoff, Xochitl Gomez as America Chavez, Benedict Wong as Wong, and Chiwetel Ejiofor.

Strange protects America Chavez, a teenager who can punch holes between universes, from Wanda Maximoff. Corrupted by the Darkhold, Wanda intends to steal America's power to reach a universe where her children exist. The chase crosses several realities, including one where Strange meets the Illuminati. Wanda destroys the Darkhold temples and apparently herself after seeing her children recoil from her.

Approximate worldwide box office: $956 million.

### Thor: Love and Thunder (2022)

Directed by Taika Waititi. Starring Chris Hemsworth, Natalie Portman as Jane Foster, Christian Bale as Gorr the God Butcher, Tessa Thompson, and Russell Crowe as Zeus.

Gorr, armed with the Necrosword, sets out to kill every god after his own deity lets his daughter die. Thor reunites with Jane Foster, now wielding a reforged Mjolnir as the Mighty Thor while terminally ill with cancer. They pursue Gorr to the realm of Eternity, where Jane dies and Gorr, granted his wish, asks instead for his daughter to be returned. Thor raises the child.

Approximate worldwide box office: $761 million.

### Black Panther: Wakanda Forever (2022)

Directed by Ryan Coogler. Starring Letitia Wright as Shuri, Angela Bassett as Ramonda, Lupita Nyong'o, Danai Gurira, Winston Duke as M'Baku, and Tenoch Huerta as Namor.

Following the death of T'Challa, Wakanda mourns its king while resisting pressure from world powers seeking vibranium. The search leads to conflict with Talokan, an underwater civilisation led by Namor. Ramonda is killed, and Shuri takes on the Black Panther mantle before negotiating peace with Namor.

The film followed the death of Chadwick Boseman in 2020, and the role of T'Challa was not recast. Angela Bassett received an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actress. Approximate worldwide box office: $859 million.

## Phase Five (2023–2025)

### Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania (2023)

Directed by Peyton Reed. Starring Paul Rudd, Evangeline Lilly, Michael Douglas, Michelle Pfeiffer, Kathryn Newton as Cassie Lang, and Jonathan Majors as Kang the Conqueror.

The Lang and Pym families are pulled into the Quantum Realm, where they find a hidden civilisation ruled by Kang the Conqueror, a variant exiled there by his own kind. Janet van Dyne, who had met and helped him decades earlier, knows what he is. Scott and Hope defeat Kang, but the film's stingers show a council of many Kang variants convening.

Approximate worldwide box office: $476 million.

### Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3 (2023)

Directed by James Gunn. Starring Chris Pratt, Zoe Saldaña, Dave Bautista, Karen Gillan, Pom Klementieff, Bradley Cooper, Vin Diesel, and Chukwudi Iwuji as the High Evolutionary.

After Adam Warlock critically injures Rocket, the Guardians must find an override key to bypass the kill switch inside him, uncovering his origin as an experiment of the High Evolutionary. The team destroys the Evolutionary's operation and rescues his captive creatures. The group disbands into new roles, with Rocket leading a reformed team and Quill returning to Earth to find his grandfather.

The final film with this iteration of the team. Approximate worldwide box office: $845 million.

### The Marvels (2023)

Directed by Nia DaCosta. Starring Brie Larson as Carol Danvers, Teyonah Parris as Monica Rambeau, Iman Vellani as Kamala Khan, and Zawe Ashton as Dar-Benn.

Carol Danvers, Monica Rambeau and Kamala Khan find their powers entangled, swapping places whenever they use them. They must learn to fight as a unit against Kree revolutionary Dar-Benn, who is tearing unstable jump points through space to restore her dying homeworld. Monica is stranded in an alternate universe sealing the resulting rift.

Approximate worldwide box office: $206 million, the lowest-grossing MCU film.

### Deadpool & Wolverine (2024)

Directed by Shawn Levy. Starring Ryan Reynolds as Wade Wilson, Hugh Jackman as Logan, Emma Corrin as Cassandra Nova, Matthew Macfadyen as Mr. Paradox, and Morena Baccarin as Vanessa.

Wade Wilson's universe is scheduled for pruning by the Time Variance Authority following the death of its "anchor being". Recruited and then discarded, he searches the multiverse for a Wolverine variant willing to help, and the mismatched pair are dumped in the Void, where they confront Cassandra Nova and a gallery of forgotten variants.

The first R-rated MCU film, and the highest-grossing R-rated film of all time. It followed Disney's acquisition of 20th Century Fox, which brought the X-Men film rights to Marvel Studios. Approximate worldwide box office: $1.34 billion.

### Captain America: Brave New World (2025)

Directed by Julius Onah. Starring Anthony Mackie as Sam Wilson, Harrison Ford as President Thaddeus Ross, Danny Ramirez as Joaquin Torres, Tim Blake Nelson as Samuel Sterns, and Carl Lumbly as Isaiah Bradley.

Sam Wilson, now Captain America without a super-soldier serum, is drawn into an international incident over a newly discovered material as Thaddeus Ross assumes the presidency. Sterns manipulates events to expose Ross, who transforms into the Red Hulk.

Harrison Ford took over the role of Ross following the death of William Hurt. Approximate worldwide box office: $415 million.

### Thunderbolts* (2025)

Directed by Jake Schreier. Starring Florence Pugh as Yelena Belova, Sebastian Stan as Bucky Barnes, David Harbour as Red Guardian, Wyatt Russell as John Walker, Hannah John-Kamen as Ghost, Julia Louis-Dreyfus as Valentina Allegra de Fontaine, and Lewis Pullman as Bob.

A group of antiheroes and expendable operatives are lured into a trap by Valentina, who is covering up her own black programmes. Forced to cooperate, they confront Bob, a test subject whose overwhelming power has a devastating depressive counterpart. The film's resolution is emotional rather than physical, and the team is publicly rebranded as the New Avengers.

Notable for its focus on depression and isolation. Approximate worldwide box office: $382 million.

## Phase Six (2025 onwards)

Phase Six is intended to conclude the Multiverse Saga. Beyond the released title below, the slate consists of announced films whose dates and details may change.

### The Fantastic Four: First Steps (2025)

Directed by Matt Shakman. Starring Pedro Pascal as Reed Richards, Vanessa Kirby as Sue Storm, Joseph Quinn as Johnny Storm, Ebon Moss-Bachrach as Ben Grimm, Julia Garner as the Shalla-Bal Silver Surfer, and Ralph Ineson as Galactus.

Set in a retro-futuristic 1960s-styled alternate universe, the film introduces Marvel's first family already established as celebrated heroes. They face the herald of Galactus and then Galactus himself, who demands their newborn child in exchange for sparing Earth.

The first Fantastic Four film produced by Marvel Studios. Approximate worldwide box office: $520 million.

### Announced upcoming films

Avengers: Doomsday, directed by Anthony and Joe Russo, with Robert Downey Jr. returning to the MCU in the new role of Doctor Doom rather than as Tony Stark. Avengers: Secret Wars is planned to follow it and close the Multiverse Saga. Further Spider-Man and other titles have also been announced. Release dates for all of these have shifted more than once and should be treated as provisional.

## The Infinity Stones

The six Infinity Stones are remnants of singularities that predate the universe. Each governs an essential aspect of existence, and together, mounted in the Infinity Gauntlet, they grant the wielder the power to reshape reality. They are the central objects of the Infinity Saga.

### Space Stone

Blue. Housed inside the Tesseract. Allows instantaneous travel anywhere and the opening of portals. It appears in Captain America: The First Avenger, The Avengers, and is taken by Thanos from the Asgardian refugee ship at the start of Infinity War.

### Mind Stone

Yellow. Concealed inside Loki's scepter in The Avengers, used to control minds. In Age of Ultron it is set into Vision's forehead and grants him life. Thanos rips it from Vision at the end of Infinity War.

### Reality Stone

Red. Takes the liquid form known as the Aether in Thor: The Dark World, where Malekith seeks it to darken the universe. Afterwards it is entrusted to the Collector, from whom Thanos takes it.

### Power Stone

Purple. Contained in the orb at the centre of Guardians of the Galaxy, sought by Ronan the Accuser. Its raw energy destroys most beings who touch it directly. Thanos takes it from Xandar before Infinity War begins.

### Time Stone

Green. Held in the Eye of Agamotto by the Masters of the Mystic Arts. Doctor Strange uses it to loop time and defeat Dormammu, and later to view millions of possible futures on Titan. He surrenders it to Thanos to save Tony Stark's life.

### Soul Stone

Orange. Hidden on the planet Vormir and guarded by the Red Skull, who was banished there by the Space Stone. It can only be obtained by sacrificing someone the seeker loves. Thanos sacrifices Gamora for it in Infinity War; Natasha Romanoff sacrifices herself for it in Endgame.

## Directors and Creative Team

Kevin Feige has produced every MCU film as president of Marvel Studios and oversees the franchise's overall direction.

Anthony and Joe Russo, known as the Russo brothers, directed four of the most pivotal films: Captain America: The Winter Soldier, Captain America: Civil War, Avengers: Infinity War and Avengers: Endgame. They are attached to Avengers: Doomsday.

Jon Favreau directed Iron Man and Iron Man 2, effectively setting the tone for the entire franchise, and appears on screen throughout the MCU as Happy Hogan.

James Gunn wrote and directed all three Guardians of the Galaxy films, giving the cosmic side of the MCU its distinctive humour and music.

Taika Waititi directed Thor: Ragnarok and Thor: Love and Thunder, reinventing Thor as a comedic character.

Ryan Coogler directed Black Panther and Black Panther: Wakanda Forever.

Jon Watts directed the three Tom Holland Spider-Man films. Peyton Reed directed all three Ant-Man films. Joss Whedon directed the first two Avengers films. Other directors include Kenneth Branagh (Thor), Joe Johnston (Captain America: The First Avenger), Scott Derrickson (Doctor Strange), Sam Raimi (Multiverse of Madness), Chloé Zhao (Eternals), Destin Daniel Cretton (Shang-Chi), Cate Shortland (Black Widow), Nia DaCosta (The Marvels), Shawn Levy (Deadpool & Wolverine) and Matt Shakman (The Fantastic Four: First Steps).

## Notable Trivia and Recurring Elements

### Stan Lee cameos

Stan Lee, the co-creator of many Marvel characters, appeared in a cameo role in nearly every MCU film from Iron Man onwards. He died in November 2018, and his final cameo appeared in Avengers: Endgame, in which he drives a car in a 1970s sequence.

### Post-credits scenes

Every MCU film includes at least one scene during or after the credits. The tradition began with Nick Fury's Avengers Initiative tease at the end of Iron Man. These scenes are typically used to introduce a character or set up the next film, and audiences have come to expect them.

### Famous lines

"I am Iron Man" is spoken by Tony Stark at the end of Iron Man and again as he snaps his fingers in Avengers: Endgame. "Avengers assemble" is deliberately withheld until the final battle of Endgame, where Steve Rogers finally says it. "I can do this all day" is Steve Rogers's recurring line. "I am Groot" is nearly the only line Groot speaks.

### The Snap and the Blip

The event in which Thanos erases half of all life is called the Snap, or the Decimation. The five-year period that follows is referred to in-universe as the Blip, and the moment when everyone returns is also called the Blip. Those who returned had not aged, creating widespread social disruption addressed in several later films and series.

### Wakanda

Wakanda is a fictional East African nation, hidden from the outside world and built on a deposit of the metal vibranium left by a meteorite. Vibranium absorbs kinetic energy and is the material of Captain America's shield. The Wakandan salute, crossed arms with the words "Wakanda forever", became widely recognised after Black Panther's release.

### Casting continuity

Robert Downey Jr. played Tony Stark from 2008 to 2019 and is returning to the franchise as Doctor Doom. Chris Evans played Steve Rogers from 2011 to 2019, handing the shield to Anthony Mackie's Sam Wilson. Mark Ruffalo replaced Edward Norton as Bruce Banner, Don Cheadle replaced Terrence Howard as James Rhodes, and Harrison Ford replaced William Hurt as Thaddeus Ross. Samuel L. Jackson has played Nick Fury across the entire run of the franchise.
